# UI Folding

> **Fold concepts, don't remove them.**

## The Problem

Most products keep adding features horizontally — new tabs, new tools, new "things" — because it feels safer than consolidating. But that's how you get bloated interfaces nobody can navigate.

## The Better Way: Fold

Fold concepts that should've been the same all along.

When you merge "issues" and "bugs" into a "database with a status property", you're not taking anything away — you're revealing the truth that was always there.

## The Trick: Layers

- People start with **simple, familiar concepts**.
- As they go deeper, they **discover the power underneath**.
- They're never overwhelmed, because they only see what they're ready for.

## The Hard Part: Transitions

People who loved the old "bugs" tab feel like you took their thing away, even though it's still there and more powerful. Guide them through the folds gently.

## Why It Matters

Single-purpose apps can't evolve — their whole identity is "we only do X". But reality is messy: concepts and roles start to blend.

The future belongs to systems like Cursor and Notion that are infinitely malleable, so they can fold and unfold to match how you actually think.

## When to Use

- Adding new features horizontally (a new tab, tool, or entity type)
- Two or more concepts in the product look like variations of the same thing
- The interface is getting bloated and hard to navigate
